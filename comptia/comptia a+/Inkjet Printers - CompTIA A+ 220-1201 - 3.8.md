# Inkjet Printers

## Overview

**Inkjet** (ink-dispersion) printers spray ink directly onto paper. They offer a good balance of cost and quality for consumer use.

### Advantages
- Relatively inexpensive technology
- Quiet operation
- High-resolution output

### Disadvantages
- Expensive ink cartridges (proprietary)
- Ink eventually fades
- Print heads clog easily

---

## Components

### Ink Cartridge
Holds colored ink that gets dispensed onto paper. Uses **CMYK** color model:
- **C** = Cyan
- **M** = Magenta
- **Y** = Yellow
- **K** = Key (black)

### Print Head
Responsible for releasing ink drops onto the page.

![[assets/comptiaA+ - InkjetPrinters1.png]]

> [!note]
> **Design variation**: Some consumer printers integrate the print head into the cartridge itself. When you replace the cartridge, you get a new print head. Other printers separate the two components, allowing cartridge replacement without print head replacement.

### Feed Rollers
Pick up and advance paper through the printer.

![[assets/comptiaA+ - InkjetPrinters2.png]]

> [!info]
> Must be kept **clean and in good condition** to avoid paper jams and misfeeds.
>
> **Duplexing**: Some printers include automatic double-sided printing capability.
