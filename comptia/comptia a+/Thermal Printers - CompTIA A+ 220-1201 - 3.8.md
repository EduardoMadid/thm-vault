# Thermal Printer

- Uses heat instead of ink
    - White paper turns black when heated
    - No ribbon, no toner, no ink
- Common as receipt or label printer
- Very quiet, almost silent
- Paper is sensitive to light, heat and clear tape
    - Stored receipts fade over time
- HEAT = BLACK PAPER

## Feed assembly

- Pulls the paper through the printer
- Relatively small paper path
- Uses friction to hold the paper in place

## Heating element

- Full-length heating element
- No moving print head
- Single piece across the width of the paper

## Thermal paper

- Paper coated with a chemical layer
    - Thermochromic paper, also called thermal paper
    - Changes color when heated
- Used in cash registers, credit card terminals and quiet areas
- Looks like normal paper but feels different to the touch
