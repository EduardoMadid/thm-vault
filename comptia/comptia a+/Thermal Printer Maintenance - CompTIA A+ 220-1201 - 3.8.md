Thermal printers have no ink, toner or ribbon. The print head heats specially coated paper to create the image, so almost all maintenance revolves around the paper and the heating element.

## Thermal paper replacement

- Relatively inexpensive
    - But impossible to substitute with regular paper
    - The coating is what makes the image appear
- Different sizes
    - Not standardized like laser printer paper
    - Receipt rolls vary in width and diameter
    - Keep a list of the sizes your devices use
- The replacement process is easy
    - Simple paper feed, drop the roll in and close the lid
    - Small device, no complex assemblies

## Cleaning the heating element

- Liquid cleaner
    - Isopropyl alcohol (IPA)
    - A cleaning pen is the practical option
    - Check the manufacturer's recommendations first
- Swab gently
    - Usually a small area to cover
    - The print head is fragile
- Use a cleaning card
    - Cleans the head and the paper pathways in one pass

## Removing debris

- Relatively small amount of residue
    - Paper bits and dust
    - No toner to deal with
- Blow out the printer
    - Compressed air, take it outside
- Wipe it out
    - Damp cloth
- Avoid using a vacuum
    - Unless it's designed for computers
        - Those resist static buildup and discharge

## Paper sensitivity to heat

- Heat is what creates the output on thermal paper
    - Extra heat will darken the entire page
- Avoid hot areas
    - Car dashboard
    - Radiator
    - Hair dryers
- Receipts also fade over time
    - Don't use thermal paper as archival media
    - Scan or photocopy anything you need to keep
