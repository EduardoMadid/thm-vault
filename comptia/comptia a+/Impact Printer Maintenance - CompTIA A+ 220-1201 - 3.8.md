Impact printers work by striking pins against a ribbon and pressing it into the paper. That physical contact is what allows multipart forms and carbon copies, and it is also what wears the parts down. Maintenance revolves around three consumables: ribbon, print head and paper.

## Printer ribbon replacement

- Single ribbon
    - Self contained cartridge
    - One long loop that keeps cycling past the print head
- Replace when the ink becomes too light
    - The ink is eventually consumed and output starts fading
- Designed to be modular
    - Replacement takes less than a minute

## Print head replacement

- Takes a lot of abuse
    - The pins hit the ribbon and paper directly, thousands of times per page
- Gets hot
    - Friction generates heat, so let it cool before touching it
- Another modular part
    - Look for a release lever on the bar that holds the head
- Replace it together with the ribbon for the best result
    - Output should look perfect after both are swapped

## Replacing paper

- Not as simple as a laser printer
    - Continuous form paper must feed perfectly onto the tractor sprockets
    - The holes on the sides have to line up with the pins
- Forms must be positioned correctly
    - Text needs to land inside a predefined space on the form
    - Common with invoices, labels and multipart receipts
- Paper must feed without constraint
    - Check that nothing blocks the path in or out of the printer
    - A stack that catches on something causes misalignment or a jam
