## Cloud computing

> [!info] Cloud is not just a server hosted somewhere else. It extends the management of apps and services.

- Effectively unlimited resources, as long as you pay for them
- Deploy an infrastructure in minutes, create and tear down on demand
- International scope, deploy in specific parts of the world
- Use only the required resources, cost is based on usage

## Deployment models

|Model|Who uses it|
|---|---|
|Public|Available to everyone over the internet|
|Private|Your own virtualized local data center|
|Hybrid|A mix of public and private|
|Community|Several organizations share the same resources|

## Service models

### Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

- Sometimes called Hardware as a Service (HaaS)
- You outsource the equipment
- You are still responsible for management and security
- Your data is out there, but more within your control
- Example: web server providers

### Software as a Service (SaaS)

- On-demand software, no local installation
- No reason to manage your own email distribution or payroll
- Central management of data and applications, your data is out there
- A complete application offering, no development work required
- Examples: Google Mail, Microsoft 365

### Platform as a Service (PaaS)

- No servers, no software, no maintenance team, no HVAC
- Someone else handles the platform, you handle the development
- You have no direct control of the data, people, or infrastructure
- Trained security professionals are watching your stuff
- Put the building blocks together, develop your app from what the platform offers
- Example: SalesForce.com

> [!note] The lower the service model, the more you manage. IaaS leaves the OS and up with you. PaaS gives you runtime and data. SaaS leaves you with almost nothing but your own data.

## Practice

> [!question]- You need to deploy an application without managing servers, patching, or the runtime, but you still write the code. Which model is this? Platform as a Service (PaaS). The provider owns the platform, you own the development.

> [!question]- Several hospitals in the same region pool resources into one shared cloud environment. Which deployment model is this? Community. Multiple organizations with a shared interest sharing the same resources.

> [!question]- Under IaaS, who is responsible for securing the operating system? You are. IaaS outsources the hardware, not the management or the security above it.
