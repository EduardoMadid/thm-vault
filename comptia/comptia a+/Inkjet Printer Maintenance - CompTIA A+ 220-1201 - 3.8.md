# Inkjet Printer Maintenance

#printers #inkjet #maintenance

## Cleaning print heads

Ink is sprayed as small droplets through very small holes in the print head, so clogs are common. Clogged heads show up in the output as streaks or sections of missing color.

- Many printers run an automatic cleaning cycle daily
- Cleaning can also be started manually from the printer menu or driver, takes a few minutes
- Some print heads and cartridges can be removed for manual cleaning

> [!question]- Output has streaks and missing color. First step?
> Run the print head cleaning cycle. If it persists, remove the head and clean it manually.

## Replacing inkjet cartridges

- Colors are usually separate: Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key (black)
- Some models combine CMY in one cartridge and keep K separate
- Swap takes seconds, the printer then spends a few minutes priming and calibrating

> [!question]- What does the K in CMYK stand for?
> Key, the black cartridge.

## Calibration

Aligns the nozzles to the paper.

- Lines should print crisp
- Colors should align with no fringing
- The printer includes a calibration option, manual adjustment may be needed after the test page

> [!question]- Colors print misaligned after a cartridge swap. What to do?
> Run the printer calibration routine.

## Clearing jams

The paper path has many turns and twists, so a jam is inevitable.

- Remove the paper tray and any loose sheets
- Pull the jammed paper out of the path with firm pressure, do not rip it
- Check the path for scraps of paper left behind

> [!question]- Why check for scraps after clearing a jam?
> Leftover pieces stay in the path and cause repeated jams.
